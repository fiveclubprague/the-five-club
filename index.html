01 / The concept

Football first.
Community always.

A reliable, year-round home for small-sided football in Prague. A place where teams can play, meet and come back regularly.

  • Indoor by design. Consistent playing conditions all year, winter included.
  • Easy to use. Online booking, organised time slots, a simple customer journey.
  • More than a pitch. A bar and lounge to stay before and after the game.
